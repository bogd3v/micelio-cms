# Authors

Moved from `CLAUDE.md` (#113); the text is unchanged.

`api::author.author` (no draft and publish) is i18n-localized for `bio` only; `name`, `avatar` and `email` are shared across locales. Strapi connects a localized article to its author in the article's locale and throws a `ValidationError` (`Document … locale "xx" not found`) when that localization is missing, so `src/utils/author-locales.ts` (Document Service middleware in `register()`) creates every other localization after an author is created or cloned (not atomic: written after the create commits) and rejects deleting a single language (a delete without locale becomes `*`), a `plugin::i18n.locale` `afterCreate` lifecycle (`bootstrap()`) fills a locale added later, and `src/migrations/author-locales.ts` (first in `bootstrap()`) creates the missing ones on boot and moves article links to the author row in the article's locale through the join table, so articles' `updatedAt` stays as it was. `email` is `private: true`: never returned, and filtering, sorting or selecting it answers 400. The frontend and build tokens have `api::author.author.find` (without it Strapi 5 strips `author` from populated articles); production's hand-made `frontend` token needs it added in the admin panel.
