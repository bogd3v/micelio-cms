# Trademarks

> **Draft, pending a lawyer's review.** Until it is approved this text states the maintainer's intent, not a legal commitment.

The AGPL gives you the right to use, change and host the code of Micelio. It grants no right to the name **Micelio** or to the Micelio logo (AGPL section 7(e)). This policy says what you may do with them. Its purpose is that people can tell the official project, hosting and theme store from the others, not to limit what you build with the software.

"Micelio" and the logos in [`brand/`](brand/) are marks of Alejandro Ramirez Garcia. The logo files are not covered by the AGPL; their terms are in [`LICENSES/LicenseRef-Micelio-Trademark.txt`](LICENSES/LicenseRef-Micelio-Trademark.txt).

## What you may do without asking

- Say, truthfully, that a site, theme, plugin, service or article **works with Micelio** or **is built on Micelio**, using the name in plain text ("a theme for Micelio", "hosting for sites built with Micelio").
- Distribute Micelio **unmodified**, under its own name.
- Write about, review or link to Micelio, and show the logo unaltered in that context when it is plain that you are talking about the project.
- Keep the name and logo where the code already shows them, such as the source link in the footer that the AGPL requires you to offer.
- Name your own site, theme or service anything that does not start from "Micelio" or suggest that it is Micelio.

## What needs written permission

- Naming a **hosting service**, a **theme store or registry**, a company, a domain or a product after Micelio ("Micelio Cloud", "Micelio Themes", `micelio-hosting.example`).
- Naming a **modified version** after Micelio. A fork keeps the code's license, but it gets its own name and says it is based on Micelio.
- Using the logo, or a mark close enough to be confused with it, on anything that **suggests the maintainer made, approved or endorses it**: official-looking hosting, stores, certifications, merchandise or sponsorship.
- Altering the logo: recolouring it, redrawing it or combining it with another mark.

To ask, open an issue titled "trademark permission". Permission is written, specific to one use, and can be withdrawn if the use changes.

## Other names

The BogDev name and logos and the Bogotá theme's mark are the maintainer's own work too and are covered the same way; see [THIRD-PARTY.md](THIRD-PARTY.md). Third-party names such as Mastodon and Strapi belong to their owners.

## Reporting a misuse

If a service or product uses the name in a way this policy does not allow, tell us through an issue. We prefer to ask first and agree on a fix.
